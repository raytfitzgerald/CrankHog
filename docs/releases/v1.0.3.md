## Google Calendar

- **Connect from the calendar.** Click **Connect Google Calendar** at the top
  of the Calendar page, or use Settings → Strava & sync, and sign in with your
  Google account. Every planned workout, run, swim and other session then
  shows as an all-day event in a new calendar named CrankHog.
- **It follows your plan.** Add, move, rename or remove a session, re-lay a
  plan or import from intervals.icu, and Google follows within seconds.
  Changes made offline go across later. An event dragged to another day in
  Google goes back to its day in CrankHog: the plan lives here.
- **What Google gets.** Each session's name, day and a short description:
  the sport, length, distance, effort, TSS or load, and your notes. Sessions
  from 30 days ago onward; events and days off aren't sent. The events show as
  free time and have no reminders, so they don't block your day or ping your
  phone.
- **What CrankHog can reach.** Only the calendar it made. It can't see or
  change your other calendars or events. Signing out leaves the CrankHog
  calendar in Google; delete it there if you no longer want it.
- **Google's warning.** Google hasn't finished reviewing CrankHog yet, so its
  sign-in page may say the app isn't verified. Choose **Advanced**, then
  **Go to CrankHog**.

The Mac app only for now: the web and iPhone builds don't have it yet.
