**If you upgraded to 0.6.0 and your rides vanished, they are not gone.** This
release gets them back, and nothing needs doing by hand.

## The rides 0.6.0 left behind

0.6.0 renamed `~/Library/Application Support/FreeGaz` to `…/CrankHog` on first
launch, so every ride, workout, route and setting would come across. It never
did. The app opened on an empty profile and looked, reasonably, like it had
thrown away years of riding.

Nothing was deleted. The old folder sat exactly where it was the whole time —
the code that moves it refuses rather than risk merging two profiles, and that
refusal is what saved it. 0.6.1 does the move properly, on the next launch,
and your history comes back.

Your exported FIT files were never affected: `~/Documents/FreeGaz` moved to
`~/Documents/CrankHog` correctly in 0.6.0.

If you already copied the folder across by hand, 0.6.1 finds the profile where
it expects it and leaves everything alone.

## Sir Loin is off the icon

The app icon is the velodrome on its own now: white boards, the four lines, the
blue infield, and nobody on the track. He was on the top straight, which worked
at 1024 px and turned to porridge at the 16 px the Dock and the menu bar
actually use.

He has not gone anywhere else. He still laps the velodrome on Home, still races
you down a route, still trots along the verge getting overtaken.

The Mac icon, the iPhone icon and the phone web app's icons are all drawn from
the same source, so they changed together.

## Everything else

No changes to riding, recording, workouts, the builder, routes, FTP tests, FIT
export, Strava, intervals.icu or the coaches.
