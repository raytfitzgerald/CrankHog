**Workouts let go of ERG when you ask them to, FTP tests put you on a hill, and
you can make your own coach.**

## Level and Slope work in workouts

The ERG / Level / Slope switch on the workout screen did switch the trainer, but
the screen never noticed. It kept showing ERG and the intensity control, so the
next press of ↑ nudged an intensity that no longer applied, and pressing M went
round in a circle that only ever reached Level. Now the switch follows a click
straight away, ↑ and ↓ move the level or the gradient you picked, and M cycles
through all three.

## ERG-off blocks have a gradient you can change

Free rides, max efforts and FTP-test efforts turn ERG off and hand the trainer
a virtual road. That road used to be dead flat with no way to change it, and
the control on screen during those blocks was intensity, which did nothing at
all there. That's how an FTP test ended at "150 %" with the legs spinning
freely.

- During an ERG-off block the control shows the gradient. ↑ and ↓ change it,
  half a percent at a time (Shift for two), and the change sticks for the rest
  of the ride, so the second effort of a 2 × 8 test starts where the first one
  ended.
- FTP-test efforts and max efforts now start on a 3 % climb instead of a flat
  road, so a strong rider sits mid-cassette rather than spinning out the big
  gear.
- A free ride block in the builder with **Flat road** turned off now means a
  3 % climb. With it on, it's still flat.
- Intensity in ERG now goes up to 200 % (it stopped at 150 %).

## Make your own coach

Settings → Coach has a **Custom coach** tile. Give them:

- a name and a photo (any picture; the middle square is used, small, and it
  stays on your Mac);
- a description of who they are, in your own words;
- lines of your own, if you like, each for a moment you pick: any time, ride
  start, hard efforts, when you fade, or when you nail it. `{power}`,
  `{targetW}`, `{remainingS}` and friends are filled in.

With an AI provider set up (Settings → AI), your AI writes a set of ride lines
from the description when you save, and the coach chat, debriefs and fresh
lines all play the character you described. Without one, your own lines carry
the ride and the plain cues fill the gaps.

The same rules hold as for the built-in coaches: no jokes about bodies, weight
or health, and swearing only as far as your Profanity setting allows. A line
that breaks the first rule is refused when you type it, with the reason.

## Cadence reads true

Cadence from a crank sensor or a power meter could read several rpm high. The
rate was worked out from the last revolution alone and then held until the
next one, and with sensors that stamp revolutions at the moment they report
them, the short gaps' high readings were held longer than the long gaps' low
ones. 90 rpm could read 95 or more. Cadence is now
measured over the last two seconds of revolutions, which reads 90 as 90.

Cadence the KICKR reports itself over FTMS was always passed through
unchanged. The KICKR estimates cadence from the flywheel rather than measuring
it, so if yours reads high with no other sensor paired, that's the trainer;
pair a cadence sensor or power pedals and CrankHog prefers them.

The live interval average now leaves out coasting, like the saved laps and the
ride average already did, so the two agree.

## The trainer lets go when the ride ends

Finishing or discarding a workout, an FTP test or a route ride was meant to
release the trainer, but the release never reached it, so it held whatever it
had last been told: the ERG target of the interval you stopped in, or the
slope of your last effort. Now it drops to the same light load as a pause.
Leaving the Just ride page with nothing recording does the same, instead of
leaving ERG or heart-rate mode running with nobody on the screen.

## Fixes found on the way

- **Intensity starts at 100 % on every ride.** A "5 % easier" from interval
  rescue, or a ↓ in one workout, used to carry into the next one.
- **Heart-rate mode no longer winds up while you're stopped.** With the rider
  stopped, heart rate falls, and the target used to climb towards 400 W to
  chase it, ready for the restart. It now holds until you're pedalling again.
- **Resuming a ramp test from a pause no longer ends it.** The "can't turn the
  pedals" rule waits until you're back above 55 rpm.
- **The phone's ±1 % buttons do nothing while ERG is off,** rather than quietly
  changing the intensity of the next ERG block.
- **The mini-HUD and the phone show your target,** not the PowerMatch-adjusted
  number the trainer is sent.
- **On a free ride with a journey, Lap and Space no longer make the map blink
  and reset its zoom.**

## Smaller things

- The big **Ride** button always takes you to "What are we riding?" when
  nothing is recording, instead of whichever ride type you set up last.
  During a ride it still takes you back to the ride.
- Connecting Strava turns **Auto-upload** on. Turn it off in Settings →
  Strava & sync if you'd rather upload by hand.
- The builder's block panel lines its labels up properly again. At some window
  sizes the labels took most of the width and squeezed the switches' text into
  a column of single words.
- The workout screen's controls no longer sit under the device chip in the
  top corner.
