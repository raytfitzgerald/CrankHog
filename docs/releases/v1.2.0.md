**Import a coach's plan from a document, and a coach chat that knows your plan.**

## Import a plan document

- **Drop in your plan.** In Workouts → Training plan → **Add a training plan**,
  choose **Import a plan document** and pick a spreadsheet (.csv, .xlsx, .xls,
  .ods, .tsv), a Word file (.docx), a PDF, or a text or Markdown file. It's
  read on your computer.
- **Your AI provider reads it into sessions.** Before anything is sent,
  CrankHog says where the document's text goes (the provider you chose in
  Settings → AI) and that nothing else goes with it: not your rides, your
  profile or any other file. Nothing is sent until you click **Send it and
  read it**. A long plan is read a few parts at a time and takes a few
  minutes.
- **Check it before it's saved.** The review lists every session it will
  make, by day, sport and length; anything it couldn't read and why; and
  anything on or after race day. A plan numbered by weeks gets a **Week 1
  starts** date you can move, and week numbers that start at 0 or count down
  to the race are put in order.
- **Its race comes with it.** A race the document names goes on your
  calendar as an A event (or the plan builds to the one already there), and
  the plan counts back from it.
- **No AI?** **Download the template**, write one session a row (date, sport,
  name, steps, notes), and it's read without AI, in the Mac, web and iPhone
  apps. Steps are written as in the
  [format guide](https://github.com/raytfitzgerald/CrankHog/blob/main/docs/WORKOUT-FORMAT.md).
- A scanned PDF is a picture of the page and can't be read: export the plan
  as text from where it was made.

## The coach chat knows your training

- **Everything in CrankHog, fresh with every message:** your plans and which
  week you're in, the calendar from four weeks back to twelve weeks ahead
  (with the next two weeks' steps), races with your projected form on race
  day, days off, and your runs, swims and other sports alongside your rides.
  Ask what's planned tomorrow and it reads your calendar instead of making a
  workout up.
- **What goes to your AI provider has changed.** Activities and planned
  workouts synced from intervals.icu now go with your chat, along with the
  rest of your CrankHog data. Strava data still never does, and neither does
  your name.
- With Ollama, long chats and plan imports no longer lose the start of the
  prompt.

## Activities and Google Calendar

- **Squash, the gym and other sports keep their full time** from a FIT
  file: they only count as stopped when your watch's timer was paused. A
  90-minute squash game no longer comes in as 13 minutes. Import an old file
  again to fix it; activities from intervals.icu or logged by hand are left
  alone.
- **Google Calendar events for runs, swims and other sessions** now carry
  the session's whole file: its header and its steps.
