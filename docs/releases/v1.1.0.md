**Build your runs and swims, pick a workout from any day, and count a plan back from race day.**

## Bike, Run and Swim

- **Three tabs.** Workouts and the builder each have Bike, Run and Swim, with
  Bike first. Bike keeps its shelves: CrankHog's workouts, your Custom
  workouts and your Training plans. Run and Swim hold your runs and swims, to
  search, plan on a day or add to a plan.
- **A run and swim builder.** Build a session step by step: sections and
  repeats, lengths in time or distance, and targets in heart rate, pace, or a
  percent of threshold pace or CSS. The time and load are worked out as you
  go, and anything to fix shows on its step. Text mode takes the session
  format, as in the
  [format guide](https://github.com/raytfitzgerald/CrankHog/blob/main/docs/WORKOUT-FORMAT.md).
- **Edit in builder.** Open any run or swim from the Run or Swim tab and
  choose **Edit in builder** to change it.

## Calendar

- **Click a day to put a workout on it.** The picker has the same Bike, Run
  and Swim tabs, folders, search and filters as the Workouts page, and opens
  on your training plan when it has workouts. Click one and it's on the day.
- **Events, days off, sessions and logging** are on the same dialog, a tab
  away. Click an event's banner to open it there.

## Training plans

- **Count back from race day.** Under **Dates** in a plan, choose **Race
  date** and set the day. The plan works out its own start, so its last
  workout is the ride day before the race, around your days off. A plan you
  import or start empty for an event counts back from race day straight away;
  one CrankHog builds for you keeps the start date you chose.
- **Browse workouts.** The Training plan tab has **Plans and calendar** for
  building plans and putting them on the calendar, and **Browse workouts** to
  search and filter a plan's workouts, numbered in plan order, and ride any of
  them.
