**CrankHog 1.0: a training calendar and plans, and a ride clock that waits for you.**

This is the first full release. Every release before it was a pre-release, so
if you turned pre-releases off in Settings → About, updates start again here.

## Calendar and training plans

- **Calendar** in the sidebar shows the month. Each planned workout shows its
  length, its average power at your FTP and its shape. A done workout turns
  green and a missed one red. Drag a workout to another day, or click it to
  ride, move or remove it. Your moves stay put when the plan changes.
- **Training plans** live on the Training plan tab. **Add a training plan**
  asks for your event and its date, your hours a week and the days you ride,
  then builds a plan from CrankHog's workouts, or with AI if you've set it up.
  A week-by-week review comes before anything goes on the calendar. Your old
  Training plan folder becomes a plan called "Training plan".
- **Events and days off:** click a day's number to mark an A, B or C event, or
  a rest, sick or travel day. Plans move around days off and stop before the
  event, and Home counts down to your next A event and shows today's workout.
- **Fitness and form** for each week, projected ahead from your plan.
- **Other sports:** a run, swim or strength session from a watch's FIT file
  shows up on the calendar with a load from heart rate. A triathlon comes in
  leg by leg. Fitness and FTP stay cycling-only.
- **intervals.icu:** brings in your planned workouts from last week and the
  next four.

## Riding

- **The ride clock waits for your first pedal stroke.** Press Start, get on,
  and the clock, the recording and the workout begin when you've pedalled for
  two seconds. Until then the trainer holds a light load, so ERG's soft start
  begins when you do. **Start now** starts it by hand on a bike that sends no
  power or cadence.
- **Strava shows the same time as CrankHog.** Pauses are closed up before a
  ride is saved, exported or uploaded, so a two-hour ride with a stop in it
  no longer shows as 2:25.
- **Countdown beeps:** three short beeps before each workout step ends, and a
  longer one as the next begins. They play with the coach muted and over
  music. They're on by default in Settings → Coach → Countdown beeps.
- **Coasting downhill keeps you rolling.** Auto-pause used to stop a route or
  journey a few seconds after you stopped pedalling, even at 45 km/h on a
  descent. It now waits until you've nearly stopped.
- **Distance comes from your power** in free rides and ERG workouts, the way it
  already did on routes. The gear you're in no longer changes how far you go,
  and a trainer that reports no speed no longer saves 0 km.

## Trainer

- **Spin-down calibration:** Devices, then Calibrate on your trainer's card,
  for trainers that support it. Wheel-on trainers need it after every
  tyre-pressure change. Closing it early tells the trainer to stop.
