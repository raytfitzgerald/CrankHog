**Heart-rate straps that are slow to connect get the time they need.**

## Connecting

A Garmin HRM 200 showed up in the Mac app's device list and then failed to
connect, while other apps on the same Mac connected to it fine. CrankHog gave
every Bluetooth step 4 seconds. That's plenty for reading or writing a value,
but not always for connecting:

- On a Mac, the app learns a device's whole list of services before it hears
  about the one it wants. A coin-cell strap with a long list and a slow link
  can take longer than 4 seconds over that.
- Turning on a strap's readings is where a strap that asks for a secure link
  gets one, and that can take a while too.

Those two steps now get up to 30 seconds. Reading and writing still give up
after 4 seconds, so a trainer that stops answering still can't stall control
during a ride.

This hasn't been tried on an HRM 200 yet. If a strap still won't connect, the
Devices page shows the error under it; please put that in the bug report.
