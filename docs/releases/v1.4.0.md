**Claude Desktop and Claude Code can use CrankHog, journeys ride on a real map, and you can ride with riders of other apps (beta).**

## Claude Desktop and Claude Code

- **Turn it on** in Settings → AI → Claude, then click **Add to Claude
  Desktop** and install it there. For Claude Code, copy the one-line command
  from the same place and run it once in Terminal. Claude Code needs Node.js
  for it.
- **Ask about your training,** and Claude reads what the coach chat sees:
  your rides, other sports, plans, races and thresholds. It can also read
  your calendar for any dates and search your workouts.
- **Ask for changes.** Claude can add, move, change or remove sessions, add
  days off and events, set your thresholds, and build a whole plan with you
  and save it, counted back from race day. It uses your Claude plan, so
  CrankHog needs no API key for this.
- **Changes apply once you allow them in Claude.** Each one is listed under
  **Changes from Claude** in Settings → AI, with **Undo**. Turn off
  **Claude can make changes** and Claude only reads.
- Claude gets what the coach chat gets: never your name, never Strava data.
  Only apps on this Mac, under your account, can reach CrankHog. If
  CrankHog is closed, Claude opens it when it needs it.

## Journeys on a real map

- On a journey, the ride screen's panel has **Ride-along**, **Map** and
  **3D** tabs. The map shows the real roads and hills, the road coloured by
  grade, your progress and today's trail. 3D shows the terrain, with a
  camera riding behind you through the bends.
- The map comes from OpenFreeMap and the AWS Terrain Tiles, so those
  services see which part of the world is on screen. **Settings → Journeys
  → Real map** chooses where it's used: the famous journeys (the default),
  imported routes too, or nowhere. Imported GPX routes stay on the drawn
  map unless you choose otherwise, since they can start at your door.
- Offline, or on a Mac without WebGL, the drawn map stands in.

## Ride with others (beta)

- Turn it on in **Settings → Ride with others**. It's off until you do, and
  nothing is sent while it's off. You then ride shared tracks with riders of
  other indoor cycling apps over
  [OpenBikeNet](https://github.com/OpenBikeNet), an open network.
- **Routes → Shared tracks** has a 250 m velodrome and Herne Hill in both
  directions. The route dialog shows who's on a track right now and asks
  for a distance rather than a lap count.
- During the ride, **On the track** lists everyone, ahead first, with their
  app, the gap, their speed and their watts, and each rider is a dot on the
  track.
- You choose the name others see, and whether to share power and cadence
  (on to start with) and heart rate (off). The server is the public
  openbikenet.cloud unless you name your own.
- It's a beta because OpenBikeNet's protocol is still a draft and may
  change. Mac app only.

## Strava

- With Journeys on, a Strava title names the journey only on a free ride. A
  workout or FTP test keeps its own name.
- The journey's line in the description now sits just above the
  CrankHog.app line.

## Swims in yards

- Steps written in yards show in yards in the builder: "300y easy", not
  "274mtr easy".
- A 1,500 yd session totals 1,500 yd, not 1,499. A yard swim already on
  your calendar keeps its old total until it's planned again.
