**FreeGaz is now CrankHog.** Same app, same rides, new name — and a hog.

## The new name

Everything you see has changed: the wordmark, the app icon, the Mac app itself
(`CrankHog.app`), the phone app, and the site, now at
[crankhog.app](https://crankhog.app).

**Your rides are safe and you don't have to do anything.** The first time 0.6.0
starts it moves `~/Library/Application Support/FreeGaz` to `…/CrankHog` and
`~/Documents/FreeGaz/Rides` to `~/Documents/CrankHog/Rides`, and carries on.
Under the hood the database keeps its old name on purpose, so every ride,
workout, route and setting comes across untouched.

If you had both versions installed at once, CrankHog leaves the old FreeGaz
folder exactly where it is rather than trying to merge the two, and uses the new
one. Nothing is deleted either way.

The iPhone app updates in place from the App Store, as usual.

## Meet Sir Loin

There is a hog now. He is the mascot, he is faster than you, and he has never
been FTP tested.

- **He laps the velodrome on Home,** up on the stayers' line, every week.
- **On a week you haven't ridden, he has the whole track to himself,** and your
  coach has opinions about that.
- **He comes past you on a route** every few minutes, with something to say about
  it. He is not drafting. He does not acknowledge your W/kg.
- **He trots along the verge behind the ride-along,** on foot, getting overtaken.
  It is the only time you beat him.
- **He is on the app icon,** on the top straight.

With Reduce Motion turned on he stops moving: his line on a route still arrives,
and he doesn't trot through the background at all.

## Everything else

No changes to riding, recording, workouts, routes, FTP tests, FIT export,
Strava, intervals.icu or the coaches. This release is the rename and the hog.
