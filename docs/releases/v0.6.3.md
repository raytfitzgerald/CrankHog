**Seventeen new coaches, and they're allowed to be mean now.**

## The new coaches

Settings → Coach has twenty-seven of them. The new ones:

- **Characters:** Fat Guy, Shrek, Slender Man (only ever whispers, always
  right behind you), The Thing Under the Trainer (it screams, louder the
  harder you go), Mr. Bananas the chimp, Angry Goose, LinkedIn Guy, The
  Infomercial Host, The Nature Narrator and Gen Z Intern.
- **Famous voices:** Hillary, Barack, Charlie Kirk, Elizabeth Holmes, Iron
  Mike, Eric Andre and DJ Khaled join Bibi and The Donald.

Each one has its own voice and about 230 lines, covering every moment of a
ride from the warm-up to the cool-down, at every spice level. The famous
voices, the goose and the chimp get talking photo heads that ride the little
bike; the rest get drawn faces. Shrek is green. Slender Man doesn't have one.

The iPhone app leaves out the famous voices, Shrek and Slender Man.

## No more topic rules

The coaches used to steer clear of bodies, weight, health, politics and a
long list of other topics. They don't any more: CrankHog is an adult app,
and the roasts go wherever the coach wants. Pick the spice and the language
with care.

One rule is left, and it now holds everywhere, Unhinged and AI-written lines
included: no coach ever tells you to hurt yourself. If your heart rate looks
wrong, or you stop suddenly in a hard effort, every coach still drops the act.

Your own coaches follow the same rule: the only line the editor refuses is
one that tells you to hurt yourself.
