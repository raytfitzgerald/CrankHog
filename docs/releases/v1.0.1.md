**Runs, swims and squash on the calendar, next to your rides.**

CrankHog now plans and logs runs, swims and other sessions (a squash game, a
gym session) as well as rides. It's still a cycling trainer: the power curve,
FTP and the AI coach read rides only.

## The calendar

- **Click a day's number** for three choices: an event or day off, as before;
  **Plan a session** of any sport; or, on a day that has been, **Log an
  activity**. A session has a sport, what it was, a time, an optional
  distance, how hard it was and notes. How hard sets the load (an easy hour is
  about 40 TSS, an hour all out about 100) unless you type a load in.
- **Weekly volume by sport:** each week and the month show Ride, Run, Swim
  and Other, with time done against time planned and the distance done.
- **Fitness, fatigue and form count every sport.** A run or a squash game adds
  its load next to your rides' TSS. The written read at the top of the
  Fitness page still covers rides only; the numbers under it count every
  sport.
- **Done turns green:** a planned run, swim or squash game turns green when an
  activity of that sport lands on its day.
- **Edit and remove:** each activity says where it came from, and the ones you
  entered by hand can be edited.

## From intervals.icu

- **Completed activities:** turn it on in Settings → Integrations, and the
  runs, swims, squash games and outdoor rides your Garmin records come in
  from intervals.icu with its load. CrankHog checks at launch and every two
  hours, and the Calendar has **Refresh activities**. The first check looks
  back 90 days.
- **Nothing counted twice:** CrankHog's own uploads, rides already in your
  history and activities you've removed stay out. A run you already imported
  from its FIT file is matched, not added again, and importing a ride's FIT
  file replaces the intervals.icu copy of it.
- **Connect Garmin to intervals.icu directly.** Activities intervals.icu got
  from Strava can't be passed on, so CrankHog only counts them and tells you
  so. CrankHog still never reads Strava itself.
- **To your watch:** a planned run or swim has **Send to intervals.icu**.
  intervals.icu puts the workout on your Garmin, if your Garmin connection
  there allows planned workouts. Moving or removing the session in CrankHog
  changes it there too.
- **Import from intervals.icu** now brings in the runs and swims you plan
  there as well.
- **Kept from the AI:** nothing from intervals.icu is ever sent to it.

These have been tested against a stand-in for intervals.icu, not yet a real
account. If something doesn't come across, please say so in a bug report.

## Run and swim workouts

- **Text files:** a planned session is a `.txt` file with a few header lines
  (sport, name, date, pool, duration, distance, effort, notes), a blank line,
  then intervals.icu's workout steps. A file named `YYYY-MM-DD_Name.txt` goes
  on that day.
- **Import sessions** on the Calendar, the new **Run & swim** tab on
  Workouts, and training plans all take them. **Paste a session** reads the
  text as you type.
- **The import report** lists each file, where it went, and every problem by
  line, for example: `"400m" is 400 minutes. For metres write 400mtr.` A file
  with errors is skipped whole.
- **Format guide:** the link next to each import button has the rules, four
  examples and **Save examples**. The same guide is in
  [docs/WORKOUT-FORMAT.md](../WORKOUT-FORMAT.md).
- **Time and load of a planned session** come from your threshold run pace
  and swim threshold pace (CSS), new in Settings → Athlete. Leave them empty
  and a distance-only session shows its distance with no time or load.
- **Runs and swims in training plans:** add them from the plan's picker or
  with **Add to a plan**. They move like rides and stay where you put them.

## Your data

Your history moves to a new storage version the first time 1.0.1 opens.
Nothing is renamed or removed. A 1.0.0 backup restores into 1.0.1, but 1.0.0
can't restore a backup made by 1.0.1.
