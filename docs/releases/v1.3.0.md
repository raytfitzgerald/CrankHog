**The coach chat does things now: changes you apply, and a whole training plan built in the conversation.**

## Changes you apply

- **Ask, and the coach proposes.** "Move Thursday's intervals to Friday",
  "I'm sick until Sunday", "lighten next week": the reply comes with a card
  listing every change, each with the coach's reason and the session before
  and after (time, distance and load), and the calendar before and after.
  Nothing changes until you press **Apply**. **Dismiss** leaves everything
  as it was, and **Undo** puts it back, even after a restart.
- **What it can change:** add, move, swap, change or remove planned
  sessions, a plan's own included; make a bike workout (saved to Custom
  workouts, with **Open in the builder** on the card) or a run or swim
  session; plan a session without steps ("squash, an hour, hard"); rest,
  sick and travel days; add, move, rename or remove a race or event, and a
  plan built to that race moves with it; log an activity; and set your FTP,
  heart rate thresholds, weight, threshold run pace or swim CSS.
- **Without being asked.** Tell the coach you're tired, sore, ill or short
  on time, and it may propose changes on its own. They still wait for your
  Apply.
- **What it won't do:** change a session you've done, plan anything on a day
  that has passed, or rewrite a session imported from intervals.icu (the
  next import would put it back). It can move or remove one, and the card
  says so.
- A session the coach changes gets its own copy, tagged `coach`, so the
  workout in your library stays as it was. A session already sent to
  intervals.icu is sent again after a change or an Undo, and Google
  Calendar follows as it always does.

## A training plan, built in the chat

- **Ask for a plan** ("a full Ironman in 36 weeks"). The coach asks its
  questions in one message: your days, hours, long days, time away, injuries,
  whatever your data doesn't already answer.
- **Then it drafts.** CrankHog writes an outline first, then every session
  four weeks at a time, with a progress bar. A long plan takes a few
  minutes. The draft card shows the phases and recovery weeks, your hours
  week by week, each sport's hours, the key sessions, and every week's
  sessions with their steps. Nothing is saved yet.
- **Tell it what to change** ("less swimming", "my long ride is Saturday")
  and only the weeks that change are rewritten.
- **Accept and add to calendar** puts every session on its date, counted
  back from race day, with the race as an A event. Undo takes it all back.
  After that, changes come as cards to apply.

## Coaches that know you

- **A master prompt for each custom coach** (Settings → Coach): up to 8,000
  characters on your goals, your injuries, your week and how you want to be
  coached.
- **Notes for every coach**, in the same place, go with whichever coach you
  pick.
- Both go to your AI provider with your chats, ride debriefs and the fitness
  overview, never with the lines spoken during a ride.

## Google Calendar

- Every ride's event now shows its steps, so a ride the coach changes shows
  its new steps there too.

## Good to know

- Claude, OpenAI and Grok propose with their own tool calls. With Ollama,
  any local model can, though a small one may struggle to write a long plan.
- Your name still never leaves your Mac, and Strava data never goes to your
  AI provider.
